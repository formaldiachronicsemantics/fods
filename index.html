About the Series

FoDS – Formal Diachronic Semantics brings together scholars interested in the exploration of the semantic and pragmatic aspects of diachronic processes in natural languages from a formal perspective.

FoDS has been meeting yearly since 2016. The next conference will take place at the University of Konstanz on March 18-19, 2027.
